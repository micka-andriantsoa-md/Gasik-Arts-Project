## Brand & Style

This design system celebrates slow luxury, ancestral weaving traditions, and ecological craft. Designed for high-end Malagasy fiber art, woven home decor, and contemporary couture objects, the interface balances high-fashion editorial elegance with organic, tactile warmth.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Modernism**:
- **Generous Canvas Spaces:** Ample warm neutral breathing room inspired by raw sands, sun-bleached linen, and unvarnished timber.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke French-Malagasy literary journals and luxury ateliers, while crisp, approachable sans-serif body copy ensures fluid interaction across global e-commerce and storytelling touchpoints.
- **Material Authenticity:** Surfaces take cues from natural raffia fibers, hand-dyed earthy ochres, and sun-baked terracotta clay. Visual metaphors avoid synthetic neon or plastic gleam in favor of grounded organic sophistication.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the endemic natural fibers and mineral soils of Madagascar:

- **Primary (`#8B4513` - Saddle Ochre / Terre Cuite):** The foundational craft tone representing sun-cured raffia dyes, aged rosewood, and dry red earth. Used for primary interactions, featured callouts, key editorial headers, and focused states.
- **Secondary (`#C19A6B` - Raphia Doré):** A golden vegetal tint echoing hand-harvested young rambo palm strands. Applied to subtle badge accents, seasonal highlights, progress indicators, and warm secondary interactive outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#5D3A1A` - Écorce Profonde):** A dense smoked-bark brown used for anchored UI moments, active selection chips, and deep structural surfaces requiring high contrast against light neutrals.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F5EFEB` - Sable d'Écru):** The foundational light canvas, evoking unbleached cotton and raw woven raffia parchment. Used across global backgrounds to avoid the sterile glare of pure cold white.
- **Neutral Dark (`#2C1B10` - Bois Brûlé):** The ultimate text color for exceptional legibility and typographic authority, replacing default stark blacks with deep warm pigment.
- **Accent Complementary (`#4A5844` - Feuillage Subtil):** An understated olive-sage green reserved for sustainability labels, artisan traceability stamps, and success feedback.

## Typography

The pairing creates an immediate dialogue between ancestral handcraft and contemporary utility:

- **Playfair Display:** Conveys the stature of an haute-couture maison. Used exclusively for brand manifestos, artwork titles, and key editorial markers. Larger weights are held at `500` or `600` to maintain delicate serifs without overpowering mobile displays.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides ergonomic, friendly reading across product specifications, narrative descriptions, cart interactions, and UI controls. Its open counters and geometric warmth prevent technical harshness.
- **Uppercase Tracking:** When `label-lg` and `label-sm` are deployed for product origin markers (e.g., "ATELIER ANTSIRABE"), wide positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) gives an engraved, luxury label appearance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model favors an **asymmetric fluid grid** structured around 12 columns on desktop (above 1024px), 8 columns on tablet (768px - 1023px), and 4 columns on mobile (under 768px).

- **Outer Margins:** Expansive outer framing (`3rem` desktop) evokes the border margins of a fine-art catalog or luxury lookbook. On mobile devices, this gracefully contracts to `1.25rem`.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Spacing leans towards deliberate breathing space. Component cards and storytelling sections use `space-xl` and `space-2xl` vertical distances to allow imagery of delicate woven textures to capture attention without UI clutter.
- **Reflow Rules:** Grid items transition from dense horizontal showcases (curated artisan collections) on widescreen viewports to stacked, editorial swipe galleries on touch devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids modern glossy glass or artificial synthetic drops. Instead, hierarchy uses **tonal layering with warm ambient diffusion**:

- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas rests on `#F5EFEB`. Elevated cards, modals, and dropdowns sit upon `#FAF7F5` or pure `#FFFFFF`, bounded by hairline boundaries (`rgba(93, 58, 26, 0.08)`).
- **Sun-Dappled Ambient Shadows:** Depth layers use multi-step diffusion tinted with primary ochre and tertiary earth pigments rather than neutral grey or black:
  - *Resting Surface:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(93, 58, 26, 0.06)`
  - *Floating Container:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 27, 16, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(93, 58, 26, 0.04)`
  - *Lifted Modal:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(44, 27, 16, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(93, 58, 26, 0.06)`
- **Tactile Boundaries:** Tactile components utilize subtle 1px border lines in warm clay tones (`rgba(139, 69, 19, 0.15)`) to maintain distinct form without visual weight.

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `2` (medium-soft curve):

- **Default Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Buttons, inputs, chips, and small selection controls carry an organic softness reminiscent of shaped and dried vegetal strips.
- **Large Panels (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Product cards, imagery masks, and notification banners introduce gentle structural corners that avoid industrial severity.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Lookbook overlays, artisan profile feature cards, and bottom sheets adopt a polished, contemporary craft contour.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#8B4513` background, `#F5EFEB` text, with `roundedness: 2` (8px). On hover, transitions gently to `#5D3A1A` with a micro-elevation gain. Uppercase label tracking (`0.05em`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a 1.5px border of `#C19A6B` and `#5D3A1A` text. On hover, fills with a light raffia tint (`rgba(193, 154, 107, 0.12)`).
- **Text / Editorial Link:** `#8B4513` text with an understated underline offset by 4px, transitioning into full color intensity on focus.

### Input Fields
- Resting on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border in `#D9C8B8`.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px border in `#8B4513` alongside an ambient halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 69, 19, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder typography is set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, styled in `#8C7D73`.

### Cards & Craft Displays
- Composed on a clean `#FFFFFF` ground layered over the `#F5EFEB` root canvas.
- Features high-resolution photography of woven palm textures with an 8px border-radius mask.
- Includes bottom content containers featuring `Playfair Display` item names, subtle gold provenance badges, and a minimalist divider rule.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selected:** Filled with `#5D3A1A`, text in `#F5EFEB`.
- **Default:** Transparent background with a 1px border of `rgba(93, 58, 26, 0.2)` and text in `#5D3A1A`.
- Ideal for categorizing fiber styles (e.g., "Raphia Teinté", "Vannerie Fine", "Tressage Ajouré").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox elements mirror the 4px curvature of basketry knots; radio elements use classic concentric circles.
- Active fill: `#8B4513` with a crisp `#F5EFEB` checkmark icon.

### Artisan Provenance Seals (Custom Component)
- Distinctive circular or pill badge containing subtle vegetal motifs, highlighting direct weaver cooperatives, certification stamps, and fair-trade sourcing across Madagascar's high plateaus.
